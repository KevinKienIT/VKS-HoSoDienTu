## 6. Quy Chuan Trang Thai

### Task

| Trang thai | Ma | Y nghia |
|------------|----|---------|
| Mo | `OPEN` | Task da tao, chua ai nhan |
| Dang lam | `IN_PROGRESS` | Agent da nhan va dang xu ly |
| Cho review | `IN_REVIEW` | Da xong, cho peer/QA check |
| Cho test | `PENDING_TEST` | Da qua review, cho test |
| Dang fix | `FIXING` | Test fail, dang sua |
| Da xong | `DONE` | Da qua tat ca vong |
| Dong | `CLOSED` | Da chuyen sang thung rac |

### Bug

| Trang thai | Ma | Y nghia |
|------------|----|---------|
| Mo | `OPEN` | Bug moi phat hien |
| Da gan | `ASSIGNED` | Da co owner |
| Dang fix | `FIXING` | Dang sua |
| Cho retest | `PENDING_RETEST` | Da sua, cho test lai |
| Da dong | `CLOSED` | Retest pass |
| Hoan | `DEFERRED` | Tam hoan, ghi ly do |

### Prompt

| Trang thai | Y nghia |
|------------|---------|
| `chua_xu_ly` | Moi, chua ai doc |
| `dang_xu_ly` | Co agent tiep nhan |
| `da_xu_ly` | Xong, doi chuyen thung rac |
| `da_luu_tru` | Da nam trong `98_thung_rac/01_lenh_da_xong` |
